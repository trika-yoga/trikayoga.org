[Word-by-word English translation by Guru Gabriel Pradiipaka](https://www.sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/en/shiva-sutras-normal-translation-complete-trika-scriptures-non-dual-shaivism-of-kashmir/545)

## Shambhava Upaya

शाम्भवोपाय {sans}

Śāmbhavopāya {trans}

Method based on Shiva's viewpoint

::: stanza 1.1

चैतन्यमात्मा ॥१॥ {sans}

Caitanyamātmā ॥1॥ {trans}

Consciousness which is omniscient and omnipotent _(caitanyam)_ (is) the Self or true nature of Reality _(ātmā)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.2

ज्ञानं बन्धः ॥२॥ {sans}

Jñānaṁ bandhaḥ ॥2॥ {trans}

The (limited or contracted) knowledge _(jñānam)_ (is) bondage _(bandhaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.3

योनिवर्गः कलाशरीरम् ॥३॥ {sans}

Yonivargaḥ kalāśarīram ॥3॥ {trans}

The source _(yoni)_ (and her) progeny _(vargaḥ)_, (along with) that whose form _(śarīram)_ is activity _(kalā)_ (are also bondage).

:::

::: stanza 1.4

ज्ञानाधिष्ठानं मातृका ॥४॥ {sans}

Jñānādhiṣṭhānaṁ mātṛkā ॥4॥ {trans}

The basis _(adhiṣṭhānam)_ of the (limited and contracted) knowledge _(jñāna)_ (is) the un-understood Mother _(mātṛkā)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.5

उद्यमो भैरवः ॥५॥ {sans}

Udyamo bhairavaḥ ॥5॥ {trans}

Bhairava --Supreme Being-- _(bhairavaḥ)_ (is) a sudden flash or elevation of divine Consciousness _(udyamaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.6

शक्तिचक्रसन्धाने विश्वसंहारः ॥६॥ {sans}

Śakticakrasandhāne viśvasaṁhāraḥ ॥6॥ {trans}

Through union _(sandhāne)_ with the collective group _(cakra)_ of powers _(śakti)_, (there is) the disappearance _(saṁhāraḥ)_ of the universe _(viśva)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.7

जाग्रत्स्वप्नसुषुप्तभेदे तुर्याभोगसम्भवः ॥७॥ {sans}

Jāgratsvapnasuṣuptabhede turyābhogasambhavaḥ ॥7॥ {trans}

(Even) during such different (states of consciousness) _(bhede)_ as waking _(jāgrat)_, dreaming _(svapna)_ and profound sleep _(suṣupta)_, there is _(sambhavaḥ)_ the delight and enjoyment _(ābhoga)_ of the Fourth State _(turya)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.8

ज्ञानं जाग्रत् ॥८॥ {sans}

Jñānaṁ jāgrat ॥8॥ {trans}

Knowledge _(jñānam)_ (is) the waking state of consciousness _(jāgrat)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.9

स्वप्नो विकल्पाः ॥९॥ {sans}

Svapno vikalpāḥ ॥9॥ {trans}

The dream state of consciousness _(svapnaḥ)_ (is) thoughts and ideations _(vikalpāḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.10

अविवेको मायासौषुप्तम् ॥१०॥ {sans}

Aviveko māyāsauṣuptam ॥10॥ {trans}

Non-discernment or lack of awareness _(avivekaḥ)_ (is) the profound sleep _(sauṣuptam)_ of Māyā --delusion-- _(māyā)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.11

त्रितयभोक्ता वीरेशः ॥११॥ {sans}

Tritayabhoktā vīreśaḥ ॥11॥ {trans}

He is a master _(īśaḥ)_ of (his) senses _(vīra)_ who is an enjoyer (of the aforesaid "ābhoga"2 or divine delight) _(bhoktā)_ in the triad (of waking, dreaming and deep sleep) _(tritaya)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.12

विस्मयो योगभूमिकाः ॥१२॥ {sans}

Vismayo yogabhūmikāḥ ॥12॥ {trans}

The stages _(bhūmikāḥ)_ of Yoga _(yoga)_ (are) a fascinating wonder _(vismayaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.13

इच्छाशक्तिरुमा कुमारी ॥१३॥ {sans}

Icchāśaktirumā kumārī ॥13॥ {trans}

The Will _(icchā)_ power _(śaktiḥ)_ (of the enlightened Yogī is) the "Splendor of Śiva" _(umā)_ (that is) Kumārī _(kumārī)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.14

दृश्यं शरीरम् ॥१४॥ {sans}

Dṛśyaṁ śarīram ॥14॥ {trans}

All phenomena (outer or inner) _(dṛśyam)_ (are) the body _(śarīram)_ (of the enlightened Yogī).

:::

::: stanza 1.15

हृदये चित्तसङ्घट्टाद्दृश्यस्वापदर्शनम् ॥१५॥ {sans}

Hṛdaye cittasaṅghaṭṭāddṛśyasvāpadarśanam ॥15॥ {trans}

Through the union _(saṅghaṭṭāt)_ of the mind _(citta)_ on the core of Consciousness _(hṛdaye)_, there is appearance _(darśanam)_ of (all) phenomena (outer or inner) _(dṛśya)_ (and even) the void _(svāpa)_ (as they are in their essential reality).

:::

::: stanza 1.16

शुद्धतत्त्वसन्धानाद्वापशुशक्तिः ॥१६॥ {sans}

Śuddhatattvasandhānādvāpaśuśaktiḥ ॥16॥ {trans}

Or _(vā)_ by union _(sandhānāt)_ with the Pure _(śuddha)_ Principle _(tattva)_, (the Yogī becomes like one in whom) the (binding) power _(śaktiḥ)_ (existing in) a limited and conditioned being _(paśu)_ is absent _(a)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.17

वितर्क आत्मज्ञानम् ॥१७॥ {sans}

Vitarka ātmajñānam ॥17॥ {trans}

Firm and unwavering awareness _(vitarkaḥ)_ (that I am Śiva is) knowledge _(jñānam)_ of the Self (ātma).

:::

::: stanza 1.18

लोकानन्दः समाधिसुखम् ॥१८॥ {sans}

Lokānandaḥ samādhisukham ॥18॥ {trans}

The Bliss (that the Yogī feels in abiding as the Knower or Perceiver) _(ānandaḥ)_ of the world (—consisting of subjects and objects—) _(loka)_ (is his) delight _(sukham)_ of Samādhi _(samādhi)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.19

शक्तिसन्धाने शरीरोत्पत्तिः ॥१९॥ {sans}

Śaktisandhāne śarīrotpattiḥ ॥19॥ {trans}

On being united _(sandhāne)_ with the Power (of Will) _(śakti)_ (there is) production or creation _(utpattiḥ)_ of bodies _(śarīra)_ (according to the Yogī's desire).

:::

::: stanza 1.20

भूतसन्धानभूतपृथक्त्वविश्वसङ्घट्टाः ॥२०॥ {sans}

Bhūtasandhānabhūtapṛthaktvaviśvasaṅghaṭṭāḥ ॥20॥ {trans}

(The other supernormal powers of the enlightened Yogī are: the power of) putting together _(sandhāna)_ existent entities _(bhūta)_; (the power of) separating _(pṛthaktva)_ them all --i.e. the existent entities-- _(bhūta)_ (and the power of) assembling --saṅghaṭṭa-- _(saṅghaṭṭāḥ)_ everything _(viśva)_ (what has been separated by space and time).

:::

::: stanza 1.21

शुद्धविद्योदयाच्चक्रेशत्वसिद्धिः ॥२१॥ {sans}

Śuddhavidyodayāccakreśatvasiddhiḥ ॥21॥ {trans}

Full acquisition _(siddhiḥ)_ of mastery _(īśatva)_ over the collective group of powers _(cakra)_ (is achieved by the Yogī) through the emergence _(udayāt)_ of Śuddhavidyā _(śuddhavidyā)_.

:::

::: stanza 1.22

महाह्रदानुसन्धानान्मन्त्रवीर्यानुभवः ॥२२॥ {sans}

Mahāhradānusandhānānmantravīryānubhavaḥ ॥22॥ {trans}

By uniting _(anusandhānāt)_ with the Great _(mahā)_ Lake _(hrada)_, (the Yogī has) the experience _(anubhavaḥ)_ of the generative source --virility or potency-- _(vīrya)_ of (all) mantra-s _(mantra)_.

:::

## Shakta Upaya

शाक्तोपाय {sans}

Śāktopāya {trans}

The Śakti's viewpoint

::: stanza 2.1

चित्तं मन्त्रः ॥१॥ {sans}

Cittaṁ mantraḥ ॥1॥ {trans}

The mind (of someone who constantly ponders over the Highest Reality) _(cittam)_ (is) the Mantra _(mantraḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 2.2

प्रयत्नः साधकः ॥२॥ {sans}

Prayatnaḥ sādhakaḥ ॥2॥ {trans}

(Zealous and spontaneous) effort _(prayatnaḥ)_ (is) effective in fulfillment _(sādhakaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 2.3

विद्याशरीरसत्ता मन्त्ररहस्यम् ॥३॥ {sans}

Vidyāśarīrasattā mantrarahasyam ॥3॥ {trans}

The (luminous) Existence or Being (of the Perfect I-consciousness) _(sattā)_, (which consists of multitude of words) whose essence _(śarīra)_ (is) knowledge (of the highest non-dualism) _(vidyā)_, (is) the secret _(rahasyam)_ of the Mantra _(mantra)_.

:::

::: stanza 2.4

गर्भे चित्तविकासोऽविशिष्टविद्यास्वप्नः ॥४॥ {sans}

Garbhe cittavikāso'viśiṣṭavidyāsvapnaḥ ॥4॥ {trans}

Mental _(citta)_ satisfaction _(vikāsaḥ)_ in (limited) mayic powers _(garbhe)_ (is) a (mere) dream _(svapnaḥ)_ (based upon) inferior _(aviśiṣṭa)_ knowledge _(vidyā)_

:::

::: stanza 2.5

विद्यासमुत्थाने स्वाभाविके खेचरी शिवावस्था ॥५॥ {sans}

Vidyāsamutthāne svābhāvike khecarī śivāvasthā ॥5॥ {trans}

On the spontaneous _(svābhāvike)_ emergence _(samutthāne)_ of the (Highest) Knowledge _(vidyā)_, (occurs) a movement in the unlimited space of Consciousness _(khecarī)_, (which is known as) the state _(avasthā)_ of Śiva _(śiva)_.

:::

::: stanza 2.6

गुरुरुपायः ॥६॥ {sans}

Gururupāyaḥ ॥6॥ {trans}

The Guru _(guruḥ)_ (is) the means _(upāyaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 2.7

मातृकाचक्रसम्बोधः ॥७॥ {sans}

Mātṛkācakrasambodhaḥ ॥7॥ {trans}

(From a pleased Guru accrues) enlightenment __(sambodhaḥ)_ regarding the group _(cakra)_ of letters _(mātṛkā)_.

:::

::: stanza 2.8

शरीरं हविः ॥८॥ {sans}

Śarīraṁ haviḥ ॥8॥ {trans}

The body (of a person into whom the aforesaid enlightenment was poured) _(śarīram)_ (becomes) an oblation _(haviḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 2.9

ज्ञानमन्नम् ॥९॥ {sans}

Jñānamannam ॥9॥ {trans}

The (limited) knowledge _(jñānam)_ (is) the food _(annam)_.

:::

::: stanza 2.10

विद्यासंहारे तदुत्थस्वप्नदर्शनम् ॥१०॥ {sans}

Vidyāsaṁhāre tadutthasvapnadarśanam ॥10॥ {trans}

On the submergence _(saṁhāre)_ of the (Pure) Knowledge _(vidyā)_, there is appearance _(darśanam)_ of mental modifications (like in a dream) _(svapna)_ arising _(uttha)_ because of it _(tad)_, (that is, "arising because of the previous submergence of the Pure Knowledge").

:::

## Anava Upaya

आणवोपाय {sans}

Āṇavopāya {trans}

The viewpoint of Anu - a limited being

::: stanza 3.1

आत्मा चित्तम् ॥१॥ {sans}

Ātmā cittam ॥1॥ {trans}

The individual Self _(ātmā)_ (is) mind _(cittam)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.2

ज्ञानं बन्धः ॥२॥ {sans}

Jñānaṁ bandhaḥ ॥2॥ {trans}

(Mind-born) knowledge _(jñānam)_ (is) bondage _(bandhaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.3

कलादीनां तत्त्वानामविवेको माया ॥३॥ {sans}

Kalādīnāṁ tattvānāmaviveko māyā ॥3॥ {trans}

Non-discrimination _(avivekaḥ)_ of (such) principles _(tattvānām)_ as Kalā _(kalā)_, etc. _(ādīnām)_, (is) Māyā _(māyā)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.4

शरीरे संहारः कलानाम् ॥४॥ {sans}

Śarīre saṁhāraḥ kalānām ॥4॥ {trans}

The dissolution _(saṁhāraḥ)_ of the parts _(kalānām)_ --of the tattva-s or principles of manifestation-- in the body --physical, subtle and causal-- _(śarīre)_ (should be accomplished by Bhāvanā or creative contemplation).

:::

::: stanza 3.5

नाडीसंहारभूतजयभूतकैवल्यभूतपृथक्त्वानि ॥५॥ {sans}

Nāḍīsaṁhārabhūtajayabhūtakaivalyabhūtapṛthaktvāni ॥5॥ {trans}

(The Yogī should bring about) dissolution (of the vital energy) _(saṁhāra)_ in the subtle channels _(nāḍī)_, conquest _(jaya)_ of the gross elements _(bhūta)_, withdrawal (of his mind) _(kaivalya)_ from the gross elements _(bhūta)_ (and) separation --pṛthaktva-- _(pṛthaktvāni)_ from the gross elements _(bhūta)_ (by means of Bhāvanā or creative contemplation too).

:::

::: stanza 3.6

मोहावरणात्सिद्धिः ॥६॥ {sans}

Mohāvaraṇātsiddhiḥ ॥6॥ {trans}

Supernatural power _(siddhiḥ)_ (occurs) because of a veil _(āvaraṇāt)_ (drawn by) Māyā or Ignorance _(moha)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.7

मोहजयादनन्ताभोगात्सहजविद्याजयः ॥७॥ {sans}

Mohajayādanantābhogātsahajavidyājayaḥ ॥7॥ {trans}

(The Yogī acquires) mastery _(jayaḥ)_ of the Natural _(sahaja)_ Knowledge _(vidyā)_ through an all-pervasive conquest _(jayāt anantābhogāt)_ of Māyā or Ignorance _(moha)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.8

जाग्रद्द्वितीयकरः ॥८॥ {sans}

Jāgraddvitīyakaraḥ ॥8॥ {trans}

(The Yogī who has attained to Sahajavidyā or Natural Knowledge is) awake and watchful _(jāgrat)_, (while) the second one, (i.e. "the world") _(dvitīya)_, (appears) as (his) effulgence of light _(karaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.9

नर्तक आत्मा ॥९॥ {sans}

Nartaka ātmā ॥9॥ {trans}

(This very Yogī is) a Self _(ātmā)_ (that is merely) a dancing actor _(nartakaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.10

रङ्गोऽन्तरात्मा ॥१०॥ {sans}

Raṅgo'ntarātmā ॥10॥ {trans}

The stage (for that Nartaka or dancing actor to play) _(raṅgaḥ)_ (is his) inner _(antar)_ soul _(ātmā)_ (which consists of the causal and subtle bodies).

:::

::: stanza 3.11

प्रेक्षकाणीन्द्रियाणि ॥११॥ {sans}

Prekṣakāṇīndriyāṇi ॥11॥ {trans}

The senses (of that Nartaka or dancing actor) _(indriyāṇi)_ (are) the spectators (of his playing) _(prekṣakāṇi)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.12

धीवशात्सत्त्वसिद्धिः ॥१२॥ {sans}

Dhīvaśātsattvasiddhiḥ ॥12॥ {trans}

By means of _(vaśāt)_ the superior spiritual intelligence _(dhī)_, (there is) the realization _(siddhiḥ)_ of the flashing, subtle and inner vibration of the perfect I-consciousness _(sattva)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.13

सिद्धः स्वतन्त्रभावः ॥१३॥ {sans}

Siddhaḥ svatantrabhāvaḥ ॥13॥ {trans}

The state _(bhāvaḥ)_ of being Independent and Free _(svatantra)_ (is) achieved _(siddhaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.14

यथा तत्र तथान्यत्र ॥१४॥ {sans}

Yathā tatra tathānyatra ॥14॥ {trans}

As _(yathā)_ there _(tatra)_, so _(tathā)_ elsewhere _(anyatra)_, (that is, "just as that independent Yogī can exhibit Freedom in his own body, he is able to do so in any other place too"; this is the sense).

:::

::: stanza 3.15

बीजावधानम् ॥१५॥ {sans}

Bījāvadhānam ॥15॥ {trans}

(That Yogī should give full) attention _(avadhānam)_ to the Seed, (in other words, "to the Highest Śakti or the perfect I-consciousness that is the source of the entire manifestation") _(bīja)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.16

आसनस्थः सुखं ह्रदे निमज्जति ॥१६॥ {sans}

Āsanasthaḥ sukhaṁ hrade nimajjati ॥16॥ {trans}

Established _(sthaḥ)_ in the power of the Supreme Śakti (or the perfect I-consciousness, which is as a "seat" for him) _(āsana)_, (the enlightened Yogī) easily _(sukham)_ plunges _(nimajjati)_ into the Lake (of the divine and immortal Consciousness) _(hrade)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.17

स्वमात्रानिर्माणमापादयति ॥१७॥ {sans}

Svamātrānirmāṇamāpādayati ॥17॥ {trans}

(That very liberated Yogī can) produce _(nirmāṇam āpādayati)_ (any forms according to) the measure or aspect of the creative Consciousness (which is his "āsana" or "seat" --see 16th aphorism--) _(svamātrā)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.18

विद्याविनाशे जन्मविनाशः ॥१८॥ {sans}

Vidyāvināśe janmavināśaḥ ॥18॥ {trans}

As long as Sahajavidyā or Śuddhavidyā, (the Natural or Pure Knowledge) _(vidyā)_, does not disappear _(avināśe)_, (the possibility of another) birth _(janma)_ (does) disappear (for that sublime Yogī) _(vināśaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.19

कवर्गादिषु माहेश्वर्याद्याः पशुमातरः ॥१९॥ {sans}

Kavargādiṣu māheśvaryādyāḥ paśumātaraḥ ॥19॥ {trans}

Māheśvarī _(māheśvarī)_ and other goddesses _(ādyāḥ)_ (who have their sphere of influence) in "ka" _(ka)_ group _(varga)_, etc. _(ādiṣu)_, (and are) the mothers _(mātaraḥ)_ of the limited beings _(paśu)_, (become his presiding deities).

:::

::: stanza 3.20

त्रिषु चतुर्थं तैलवदासेच्यम् ॥२०॥ {sans}

Triṣu caturthaṁ tailavadāsecyam ॥20॥ {trans}

The fourth state of consciousness, (which is a Witness) _(caturtham)_, should be poured _(āsecyam)_ like _(vat)_ (a continuous flow of) oil _(taila)_ into (the other) three _(triṣu)_, (that is, in waking, dreaming and deep sleep).

:::

::: stanza 3.21

मग्नः स्वचित्तेन प्रविशेत् ॥२१॥ {sans}

Magnaḥ svacittena praviśet ॥21॥ {trans}

One should enter (that fourth state of consciousness --see aphorism 20--) _(praviśet)_ by being immersed (into it) _(magnaḥ)_ with one's own _(sva)_ mind _(cittena)_ (which must be devoid of any thought, obviously).

:::

::: stanza 3.22

प्राणसमाचारे समदर्शनम् ॥२२॥ {sans}

Prāṇasamācāre samadarśanam ॥22॥ {trans}

When a slow but firm spreading out _(samācāre)_ of the (Yogī's) vital energy _(prāṇa)_ (occurs), there is equable _(sama)_ vision _(darśanam)_, (that is, the Yogī realizes unity underlying all).

:::

::: stanza 3.23

मध्येऽवरप्रसवः ॥२३॥ {sans}

Madhye'varaprasavaḥ ॥23॥ {trans}

In the intervening stage, (that is, neither at the initial nor at the final stage of waking, dreaming and dream sleep) _(madhye)_, there is generation _(prasavaḥ)_ of inferior (mental states) _(avara)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.24

मात्रास्वप्रत्ययसन्धाने नष्टस्य पुनरुत्थानम् ॥२४॥ {sans}

Mātrāsvapratyayasandhāne naṣṭasya punarutthānam ॥24॥ {trans}

When there is union _(sandhāne)_ between the real I-consciousness _(svapratyaya)_ and the objects _(mātrā)_, (there is also) reappearance _(punar utthānam)_ (of the Bliss of that fourth state of consciousness which had) disappeared _(naṣṭasya)_ (due to the arising of the aforesaid inferior states of mind).

:::

::: stanza 3.25

शिवतुल्यो जायते ॥२५॥ {sans}

Śivatulyo jāyate ॥25॥ {trans}

(That superb Yogī who has attained to the fourth state) becomes _(jāyate)_ equal _(tulyaḥ)_ to Śiva _(śiva)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.26

शरीरवृत्तिर्व्रतम् ॥२६॥ {sans}

Śarīravṛttirvratam ॥26॥ {trans}

Remaining _(vṛttiḥ)_ in the body _(śarīra)_ (is his) vow _(vratam)_, (that is to say, he retains a physical form on account of his enormous compassion to humankind; it is really a pious act on his part).

:::

::: stanza 3.27

कथा जपः ॥२७॥ {sans}

Kathā japaḥ ॥27॥ {trans}

(His) conversation _(kathā)_ (is) the muttering (of a Mantra or prayer) _(japaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.28

दानमात्मज्ञानम् ॥२८॥ {sans}

Dānamātmajñānam ॥28॥ {trans}

Knowledge _(jñānam)_ of the Self _(ātma)_ (is his) gift _(dānam)_ (for us all).

:::

::: stanza 3.29

योऽविपस्थो ज्ञाहेतुश्च ॥२९॥ {sans}

Yo'vipastho jñāhetuśca ॥29॥ {trans}

He who _(yaḥ)_ (is) established (in the group of powers or Śakticakra) _(avipasthaḥ)_ (is) indeed _(ca)_ a means _(hetuḥ)_ of wisdom _(jñā)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.30

स्वशक्तिप्रचयोऽस्य विश्वम् ॥३०॥ {sans}

Svaśaktipracayo'sya viśvam ॥30॥ {trans}

The universe _(viśvam)_ (is) the expansion or unfoldment _(pracayaḥ)_ of his own _(sva... asya)_ Power _(śakti)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.31

स्थितिलयौ ॥३१॥ {sans}

Sthitilayau ॥31॥ {trans}

Both the maintenance (of the universe) _(sthiti)_ (and its) reabsorption _(layau)_ (are also the unfoldment of his Power).

:::

::: stanza 3.32

तत्प्रवृत्तावप्यनिरासः संवेत्तृभावात् ॥३२॥ {sans}

Tatpravṛttāvapyanirāsaḥ saṁvettṛbhāvāt ॥32॥ {trans}

Even though _(api)_ there may be occurrence _(pravṛttau)_ of those (three previous processes, viz. manifestation, maintenance and reabsorption of the universe) _(tad)_, there is no break _(anirāsaḥ)_ (in the inner state of the great Yogī) because of (his) condition _(bhāvāt)_ as the (Supreme) Knower _(saṁvettṛ)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.33

सुखदुःखयोर्बहिर्मननम् ॥३३॥ {sans}

Sukhaduḥkhayorbahirmananam ॥33॥ {trans}

(This sublime Yogī) considers _(mananam)_ pleasure and pain _(sukhaduḥkhayoḥ)_ as something external _(bahis)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.34

तद्विमुक्तस्तु केवली ॥३४॥ {sans}

Tadvimuktastu kevalī ॥34॥ {trans}

(As the noble Yogī) is completely free _(vimuktaḥ)_ from that --pleasure and pain-- _(tad)_, (he is) alone _(kevalī)_ then _(tu)_, (in short, he has attained to his own Self who is both an "only" Knower and an "only" Mass of pure Consciousness).

:::

::: stanza 3.35

मोहप्रतिसंहतस्तु कर्मात्मा ॥३५॥ {sans}

Mohapratisaṁhatastu karmātmā ॥35॥ {trans}

However _(tu)_, one who is a compact mass _(pratisaṁhataḥ)_ of delusion _(moha)_ (is merely) involved _(ātmā)_ in actions _(karma)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.36

भेदतिरस्कारे सर्गान्तरकर्मत्वम् ॥३६॥ {sans}

Bhedatiraskāre sargāntarakarmatvam ॥36॥ {trans}

When the difference _(bheda)_ disappears _(tiraskāre)_, the capacity to perform _(karmatvam)_ another _(antara)_ Creation _(sarga)_ (appears in the enlightened Yogī).

:::

::: stanza 3.37

करणशक्तिः स्वतोऽनुभवात् ॥३७॥ {sans}

Karaṇaśaktiḥ svato'nubhavāt ॥37॥ {trans}

(Anyone can realize his) creative _(karaṇa)_ power _(śaktiḥ)_ from his own _(svataḥ)_ experience _(anubhavāt)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.38

त्रिपदाद्यनुप्राणनम् ॥३८॥ {sans}

Tripadādyanuprāṇanam ॥38॥ {trans}

(There should be) enlivening _(anuprāṇanam)_ of the three states --manifestation, maintenance and reabsorption-- _(tri-pada)_ by the main one _(ādi)_ (in sum, "by the fourth state of consciousness which is a Witness to the other three ones and is full of transcendental Bliss").

:::

::: stanza 3.39

चित्तस्थितिवच्छरीरकरणबाह्येषु ॥३९॥ {sans}

Cittasthitivaccharīrakaraṇabāhyeṣu ॥39॥ {trans}

As (in the case) _(vat)_ of the mental _(citta)_ states _(sthiti)_, (so also regarding) the body _(śarīra)_, organs of sense _(karaṇa)_ and external objects --bāhya-- _(bāhyeṣu)_, _(there should be an "enlivening or vivification" by infusing them with the Bliss of the fourth state of consciousness)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.40

अभिलाषाद्बहिर्गतिः संवाह्यस्य ॥४०॥ {sans}

Abhilāṣādbahirgatiḥ saṁvāhyasya ॥40॥ {trans}

Because of the desire based on a feeling of want _(abhilāṣāt)_, there is extroversion _(bahirgatiḥ)_ of the limited being _(saṁvāhyasya)_ (who is thus subject to the wheel of Saṁsāra or Transmigration from a form of existence to another).

:::

::: stanza 3.41

तदारूढप्रमितेस्तत्क्षयाज्जीवसङ्क्षयः ॥४१॥ {sans}

Tadārūḍhapramitestatkṣayājjīvasaṅkṣayaḥ ॥41॥ {trans}

(Nevertheless,) in the case of (the great Yogī) whose awareness _(pramiteḥ)_ (is) established _(ārūḍha)_ in That --in the fourth state or Turya-- _(tad)_, with the removal _(kṣayāt)_ of that (desire) _(tad)_ (there is also) complete removal _(saṅkṣayaḥ)_ of (the condition of) limited being _(jīva)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.42

भूतकञ्चुकी तदा विमुक्तो भूयः पतिसमः परः ॥४२॥ {sans}

Bhūtakañcukī tadā vimukto bhūyaḥ patisamaḥ paraḥ ॥42॥ {trans}

Then, (when the desire finally disappears) _(tadā)_, (that Yogī uses the body which is composed of) gross elements _(bhūta)_ as covering _(kañcukī)_; (and being) completely liberated _(vimuktaḥ)_, (since he is) pre-eminently _(bhūyas)_ equal _(samaḥ)_ to Lord (Śiva) _(pati)_, (he is) perfect and full _(paraḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.43

नैसर्गिकः प्राणसम्बन्धः ॥४३॥ {sans}

Naisargikaḥ prāṇasambandhaḥ ॥43॥ {trans}

The link or association _(sambandhaḥ)_ of the vital energy _(prāṇa)_ (with the body is) natural _(naisargikaḥ)_.

:::

::: stanza 3.44

नासिकान्तर्मध्यसंयमात् किमत्र सव्यापसव्यसौषुम्नेषु ॥४४॥ {sans}

Nāsikāntarmadhyasaṁyamāt kimatra savyāpasavyasauṣumneṣu ॥44॥ {trans}

(There is vital energy --prāṇaśakti or prāṇa--) in the left subtle channel --Iḍā-- _(apasavya)_, in the right subtle channel --Piṅgalā-- _(savya)_ (and) in Suṣumnā --the middle one-- _(sauṣumneṣu)_.

By the intense and constant awareness _(saṁyamāt)_ of the center --that is, the perfect I-consciousness-- _(madhya)_ of the inner aspect _(antar)_ of (the aforesaid) prāṇaśakti _(nāsikā)_, (the Yogī abides in the constant awareness of the supreme and perfect I-consciousness forever).

What else (could one say) _(kim)_ in this respect _(atra)_?

:::

::: stanza 3.45

भूयः स्यात्प्रतिमीलनम् ॥४५॥ {sans}

Bhūyaḥ syātpratimīlanam ॥45॥ {trans}

(Regarding the enlightened Yogī), there is _(syāt)_ over and over again _(bhūyas)_ the awareness of the Supreme Self both internally and externally _(pratimīlanam)_.
