::: stanza 1

यदयमनुत्तरमूर्तिर्नजेच्छयाखिलमिदं जगत्स्रष्टुम्।   
पस्पन्दे स स्पन्दः प्रथमः शिवतत्त्वमुच्यते तज्ज्ञैः॥१॥ {sans}

Yadayamanuttaramūrtirnijecchayākhilamidaṁ jagatsraṣṭum|   
Paspande sa spandaḥ prathamaḥ śivatattvamucyate tajjñaiḥ||1|| {trans}

This very _(yad ayam)_  Anuttaramūrti --lit. That whose form is the Highest Reality-- _(anuttara-mūrtiḥ)_, according to His own desire _(nija-icchayā)_  to manifest _(sraṣṭum)_  this  _(idam)_  entire _(akhilam)_  world _(jagat)_ , vibrated _(paspande)_. That _(saḥ)_  first _(prathamaḥ)_  Vibration _(spandaḥ)_  is said to be _(ucyate)_  the Śiva-tattva --lit. the category called Śiva-- _(śiva-tattvam)_  by the ones who know _(jñaiḥ)_  that <i>(Anuttaramūrti)</i> _(tad)_ ||1||

::: stanza 2

इच्छा सैव स्वच्छा सन्ततसमवायिनी सती शक्तिः।    
सचराचरस्य जगतो बीजं निखिलस्य निजनिलीनस्य॥२॥ {sans}

Icchā saiva svacchā santatasamavāyinī sati śaktiḥ |   
Sacarācarasya jagato bījaṁ nikhilasya nijanilīnasya ||2|| {trans}

Śakti --the second tattva-- _(śaktiḥ)_ is _(satī)_  just _(eva)_ that _(sā)_  very transparent _(svacchā)_  Will _(icchā)_ <i>(that)</i> is continuously _(santata)_  multitudinous _(samavāyinī)_ . <i>(She is)</i> the Seed _(bījam)_  of    the entire _(nikhilasya)_  world _(jagataḥ)_     <i>(which)</i> comprehends <i>(everything)</i> moving _(sacara)_  <i>(and)</i>    motionless _(acarasya)_  <i>(and is)</i> hidden _(nilīnasya)_  in Herself _(nija)_ ||2||

::: stanza 3

स्वेच्छाशक्त्युद्गीर्णं जगदात्मतया समाच्छाद्य।   
निवसन् स एव निखिलानुग्रहनिरतः सदाशिवोऽभिहितः॥३॥ {sans}

Svecchāśaktyudgīrṇaṁ jagadātmatayā samācchādya |   
Nivasan sa eva nikhilānugrahanirataḥ sadāśivo'bhihitaḥ ||3|| {trans}


Having totally covered _(samāchādya)_  the world _(jagat)_ , <i>(which was)</i> emitted _(udgīrṇam)_  by His own _(sva)_  Icchāśakti --Power of Will-- _(icchā-śakti)_ , with the Self --i.e. after having covered it with the "Aham" or "I" portion-- _(ātmatayā)_ , He _(saḥ eva)_  is known as< _(abhihitaḥ)_  Sadāśiva --the third tattva or category-- _(sadāśivaḥ)_  who abides _(nivasan)_  completely _(nikhila)_  devoted _(nirataḥ)_  to confer divine Grace _(anugraha)_ ||3||

::: stanza 4

विश्वं पश्चात्पश्यनिदन्तया निखिलमीश्वरो जातः।     
सा भवति शुद्धविद्या येदन्ताहन्तयोरभेदमतिः॥४॥  {sans}

Viśvaṁ pañcātpaśyanidantayā nikhilamīśvaro jātaḥ|     
Sā bhavati śuddhavidyā yedantāhantayorabhedamatiḥ ||4|| {trans}

Afterward _(paścāt)_ , when He --the Supreme Lord-- sees _(paśyan)_  the whole _(nikhilam)_  universe _(viśvam)_  as "Idam" or "This" _(idantayā)_ --i.e.when "Idam" predominates over "Aham" or "I"--, the Īśvara-tattva --the fourth category-- _(īśvara)_  is born _(jātaḥ)_ . The Śuddhavidyā-tattva --the fifthcategory-- _(śuddhavidyā)_  is _(bhavati)_  that _(sā)_  which _(yā)_  <i>(is characterized by)</i>perception _(matiḥ)_  of unity _(abheda)_  between thestates of Aham or I-ness _(ahantayoḥ)_  <i>(and)</i> Idam or This --theuniverse-- _(idantā)_ ||4||

::: stanza 5

माया विभेदबुद्धिर्निजांशजातेषु निखिलजीवेषु।     
नित्यं तस्य निरङ्कुशविभवं वेलेव वारिधे रुन्धे॥५॥ {sans}

Māyā vibhedabuddhirnijāṁśajāteṣu nikhilajīveṣu|     
Nityaṁ tasya niraṅkuśavibhavaṁ veleva vāridhe rundhe ||5|| {trans}


Māyā --viz. the Māyā-tattva or sixth category--_(māyā)_ ,whose intention _(buddhiḥ)_  <i>(is bringing about)</i> separation _(vibheda)_  in all _(nikhila)_  living beings _(jīveṣu)_ who are born _(jāteṣu)_  as portions _(aṁśa)_  of herself --that is, of Māyā-- _(nija)_ ,always _(nityam)_  restrains _(rundhe)_  His _(tasya)_  independent _(niraṅkuśa)_  Power _(vibhavam)_  --i.e. Svātantrya or Absolute Freedom of the Lord-- like _(iva)_  the shore _(velā)_  of an ocean _(vāridheḥ)_ ||5||

::: stanza 6

स तया परिमितमूर्तिः सङ्कुचितसमस्तशक्तिरेष पुमान्।     
रविरिव सन्ध्यारक्तः संहृतशक्तिः स्वभासनेऽप्यपटुः॥६॥ {sans}


Sa tayā parimitamūrtiḥ saṅkucitasamastaśaktireṣa pumān|     
Raviriva sandhyāraktaḥ saṁhṛtaśaktiḥ svabhāsane'pyapaṭuḥ ||6|| {trans}


This very _(saḥ... eṣaḥ)_  man --in the sense of human    being-- _(pumān)_ , whose form _(mūrtiḥ)_  is    mensurable --viz. limited-- _(parimita)_ , has all _(samasta)_  his power _(śaktiḥ)_ contracted _(saṅkucita)_  on account of that <i>(Māyā)</i> _(tayā)_ . Just as _(iva)_  the sun _(raviḥ)_ , reddish _(āraktaḥ)_  during twilight _(sandhyā)_  <i>(and)</i>    with its power _(śaktiḥ)_  restrained _(saṁhṛta)_ , is    not capable <i>(of giving the same light as before)</i> _(apaṭuḥ)_  <i>(at that        time,)</i> though _(api)_  it is self-effulgent _(sva-bhāsane)_ ||6||

::: stanza 7

सम्पूर्णकर्तृताद्या बह्व्यः सन्त्यस्य शक्तयस्तस्य।     
सङ्कोचात्सङ्कुचिताः कलादिरूपेण रूढयन्त्येवम्॥७॥ {sans}


Sampūrṇakartṛtādyā bahvyaḥ santyasya śaktayastasya|     
Saṅkocātsaṅkucitāḥ kalādirūpeṇa rūḍhayantyevam ||7|| {trans}


The many _(bahvyaḥ)_  powers _(śaktayaḥ)_  of that _(tasya)_  Bountiful and Benevolent One _(santyasya)_ , viz. omnipotence _(sampūrṇa-kartṛtā)_ ,etc. _(ādyāḥ)_ , being shrunk _(saṅkucitāḥ)_  becauseof saṅkoca or contraction _(saṅkocāt)_ , are thus developed _(rūḍhayanti evam)_  into the form _(rūpeṇa)_  of Kalā --the seventh tattva or category-- _(kalā)_, etc. _(ādi)_ ||7||

::: stanza 8

तत्सर्वकर्तृता सा सङ्कुचिता कतिपयार्थमात्रपरा।     
किञ्चित्कर्तारममुं कलयन्ती कीर्त्यते कला नाम॥८॥ {sans}

Tatsarvakartṛtā sā saṅkucitā katipayārthamātraparā|     
Kiñcitkartāramamuṁ kalayantī kīrtyate kalā nāma ||8|| {trans}

That _(sā)_  Omnipotence _(sarvakartṛtā)_  of His _(tad)_ , being contracted _(saṅkucitā)_ , is merely occupied _(mātra-parā)_  insome _(katipaya)_  objects _(artha)_  --i.e. it doesnot look like opulent--. <i>(Such a contracted Omnipotence)</i> is mentioned _(kīrtyate)_  by the name _(nāma)_  of Kalā _(kalā)_  <i>(because)</i> it impels _(kalayantī)_  that _(amum)_  limited _(kiñcid)_  doer _(kartāram)_ ||8||

::: stanza 9

सर्वज्ञतास्य शक्तिः परिमिततनुरल्पवेद्यमात्रपरा।     
ज्ञानमुत्पादयन्ती विद्येति निगद्यते बुधैराद्यैः॥९॥ {sans}


Sarvajñatāsya śaktiḥ parimitatanuralpavedyamātraparā|     
Jñānamutpādayantī vidyeti nigadyate budhairādyaiḥ ||9|| {trans}


His _(asya)_  Omniscience _(sarvajñatā... śaktiḥ)_ , having a limited _(parimita)_  form _(tanuḥ)_ , is merely occupied _(mātra-parā)_  with afew and little _(alpa)_  knowables --i.e. objects-- _(vedya)_ . <i>(Such a contracted Omniscience)</i> is called _(nigadyate)_ "Vidyā" _(vidyā iti)_  by the first _(ādyaiḥ)_ sages _(budhaiḥ)_ , <i>(because)</i> it brings about _(utpādayantī)_  knowledge _(jñānam)_ ||9||

::: stanza 10

नित्यपरिपूर्णतृप्तिः शक्तिस्तस्यैव परिमिता तु सती।     
भोगेषु रञ्जयन्ती सततममुं रागतत्त्वतां याता॥१०॥ {sans}


Nityaparipūrṇatṛptiḥ śaktistasyaiva parimitā tu satī|     
Bhogeṣu rañjayantī satatamamuṁ rāgatattvatāṁ yātā ||10|| {trans}

<i>(Now,)</i> His _(tasya eva)_  Power _(śaktiḥ)_  <i>(called)</i> "Satisfaction _(tṛptiḥ)_ <i>(which is)</i> eternal _(nitya)_  <i>(and)</i> perfect _(paripūrṇa)_ " --i.e. Icchāśakti or Power of Will-- is _(satī)_ certainly _(tu)_  limited _(parimitā)_ . <i>(This    contracted Power of Will)</i> becomes the tattva or category _(tattvatām yātā)_ <i>(known as)</i> Rāga _(rāga)_  <i>(because it)</i> constantly _(satatam)_  causes that <i>(limited being)</i> to be attracted _(rañjayantī... amum)_  by enjoyments _(bhogeṣu)_ ||10||

::: stanza 11

सा नित्यतास्य शक्तिर्निकृष्य निधनोदयप्रदानेन।     
नियतपरिच्छेदकरी कॢप्ता स्यात्कालतत्त्वरूपेण॥११॥ {sans}

Sā nityatāsya śaktirnikṛṣya nidhanodayapradānena|     
Niyataparicchedakarī kḷptā syātkālatattvarūpeṇa ||11|| {trans}


That _(sā)_  Eternity _(nityatā...    śaktiḥ)_  of His _(asya)_ , being dragged down _(nikṛṣya)_  because of <i>(His)</i> act of giving _(pradānena)_  rise _(udaya)_  to destruction _(nidhana)_ , makes _(karī)_  limited regular _(niyata)_  divisions _(pariccheda)_ , <i>(and thus such an Eternity)</i> is _(syāt)_  arranged and trimmed _(kḷptā)_  in the form _(rūpeṇa)_  of the tattva or category _(tattva)_ <i>(called)</i> Kāla _(kāla)_ ||11||

::: stanza 12

यास्य स्वतन्त्रताख्या शक्तिः सङ्कोचशालिनी सैव।     
कृत्याकृत्येष्ववशं नियतममुं नियमयन्त्यभून्नियतिः॥१२॥ {sans}


Yāsya svatantratākhyā śaktiḥ saṅkocaśālinī saiva|     
Kṛtyākṛtyeṣvavaśaṁ niyatamamuṁ niyamayantyabhūnniyatiḥ ||12|| {trans}


<i>(When)</i> that _(sā eva)_  Power _(śaktiḥ)_  of His _(asya)_ , which _(yā)_  is known as _(ākhyā)_  Absolute Freedom _(svatantratā)_  --viz. Cicchakti or the Power of Consciousness--, is full _(śālinī)_  of contraction _(saṅkoca)_ , <i>(then)</i>,by restraining _(niyamayantī)_  that <i>(limited individual)</i> _(amum)_  who is devoid of free will _(avaśam)_ <i>(and)</i> restricted _(niyatam)_  regarding what is to be done _(kṛtya)_  <i>(and)</i> what is not to be done --akṛtya-- _(akṛtyeṣu)_  --i.e. he depends on right and wrong actions--, <i>(such a Power)</i> became _(abhūt)_  <i>(the tattva or category called)</i> Niyati _(niyatiḥ)_ ||12||

::: stanza 13-14

इच्छादित्रिसमष्टिः शक्तिः शान्तास्य सङ्कुचद्रूपा।     
सङ्कलितेच्छाद्यात्मकसत्त्वादिकसाम्यरूपिणी तु सती॥१३॥ {sans}

बुद्ध्यादिसामरस्यस्वरूपचित्तात्मिका मता प्रकृतिः।     
इच्छास्य रजोरूपाहङ्कृतिरासीदहम्प्रतीतिकरी॥१४॥ {sans}

Icchāditrisamaṣṭiḥ śaktiḥ śāntāsya saṅkucadrūpā|     
Saṅkalitecchādyātmakasattvādikasāmyarūpiṇī tu satī ||13|| {trans}

Buddhyādisāmarasyasvarūpacittātmikā matā prakṛtiḥ|     
Icchāsya rajorūpāhaṅkṛtirāsīdahampratītikarī ||14|| {trans}

His _(asya)_  Pacific _(śāntā)_  Power _(śaktiḥ)_  whose nature _(rūpā)_  is to contract _(saṅkucat)_ , <i>(appears)</i> as a triad _(tri-samaṣṭiḥ)_  composed of Icchā or Will _(icchā)_ ,etc. --viz. Knowledge and Action-- _(saṅkucat)_ . She --i.e. that Pacific Śakti orPower having become Prakṛti or the thirteenth tattva or category-- is _(rūpiṇī...    satī)_  certainly _(tu)_  an equipoise _(sāmya)_  of Sattva _(sattva)_ , etc. --viz. Rajas and Tamas-- _(ādika)_ , which consist _(ātmaka)_  of Will _(icchā)_ , etc. _(ādi)_  being heaped together _(saṅkalita)_ ||13||

Prakṛti _(prakṛtiḥ)_  is considered _(matā)_  as of the nature _(ātmikā)_  of Citta orlimited mind _(citta)_  whose essence _(sva-rūpa)_  isa homogeneity _(sāmarasya)_  of Buddhi --intellect-- _(buddhi)_ , etc. --viz. Ahaṅkāra and Manas-- _(ādi)_ . His --i.e. of theLord-- _(asya)_  Will _(icchā)_ , in the form _(rūpā)_  of Rajas _(rajas)_ , became _(āsīt)_  the concept of individuality _(ahaṅkṛtiḥ)_ ,which brings about _(karī)_ I-sense --i.e. ego-- _(aham-pratīti)_ ||14||

::: stanza 15

ज्ञानापि सत्त्वरूपा निर्णयबोधस्य कारणं बुद्धिः।     
तस्य क्रिया तमोमयमूर्तिर्मन उच्यते विकल्पकरी॥१५॥ {sans}

Jñānāpi sattvarūpā nirṇayabodhasya kāraṇaṁ buddhiḥ|     
Tasya kriyā tamomayamūrtirmana ucyate vikalpakarī ||15||  {trans}

Knowledge _(jñāna)_ too _(api)_, in the form _(rūpā)_ of Sattva _(sattva)_, <i>(is)</i> the cause _(kāraṇam)_ of the consciousness _(bodhasya)_ of determination _(nirṇaya)_ <i>(and this is)</i> Buddhi or intellect _(buddhiḥ)_. Its _(tasya)_ activity _(kriyā)_, whose form _(mūrtiḥ)_ is composed _(mayam)_ of Tamas _(tamas)_, is called _(ucyate)_ Manas or mind _(manaḥ)_, which produces _(karī)_ thought-constructs _(vikalpa)_ ||15||

::: stanza 16

वामादिपञ्चभेदः स एव सङ्कुचितविग्रहो देवः।     
ज्ञानक्रियोपरागप्राधान्याद्विविधविषयरूपोऽभूत्॥१६॥ {sans}

Vāmādipañcabhedaḥ sa eva saṅkucitavigraho devaḥ|     
Jñānakriyoparāgaprādhānyādvividhaviṣayarūpo'bhūt ||16||  {trans}

The fivefold _(pañca)_ division _(bhedaḥ)_ into Vāmā _(vāmā)_, etc. _(ādi)_ <i>(viz. the five buddhīndriyas or organs of cognition)</i> is _(saḥ eva)_ God _(devaḥ)_ <i>(Himself with)</i> a contracted _(saṅkucita)_ body _(vigrahaḥ)_. He became _(abhūt)_ the form _(rūpaḥ)_ of various _(vividha)_ objects _(viṣaya)_ due to the preponderance _(prādhānyāt)_ of being colored _(uparāga)_ by knowledge _(jñāna)_ <i>(and)</i> action _(kriyā)_ ||16||

::: stanza 17

श्रोत्रं चक्षुः स्पर्शनजिह्वाघ्राणानि बोधकरणानि।     
शब्दस्पर्शौ रूपं रसगन्धौ चेति भूतसूक्ष्माणि॥१७॥ {sans}

Śrotraṁ cakṣuḥ sparśanajihvāghrāṇāni bodhakaraṇāni|     
Śabdasparśau rūpaṁ rasagandhau ceti bhūtasūkṣmāṇi ||17||  {trans}

The ear _(śrotram)_, the eye _(cakṣuḥ)_, the skin _(sparśana)_, the tongue _(jihvā)_ <i>(and)</i> the nose _(ghrāṇāni)_ <i>(are)</i> the instruments _(karaṇāni)_ of consciousness _(bodha)_. Sound _(śabda)_, touch _(sparśau)_, form _(rūpam)_, taste _(rasa)_ <i>(and)</i> smell _(gandhau)_ --and _(ca)_ so on _(iti)_-- <i>(are)</i> the subtle _(sūkṣmāṇi)_ elements _(bhūta)_ ||17||

::: stanza 18

अयमेवातिनिकृष्टो जातो भूतात्मनापि भूतेशः।     
गगनमनिलश्च तेजः सलिलं भूमिश्च पञ्चभूतानि॥१८॥ {sans}

Ayamevātinikṛṣṭo jāto bhūtātmanāpi bhūteśaḥ|     
Gaganamanilaśca tejaḥ salilaṁ bhūmiśca pañcabhūtāni ||18||  {trans}

This very One _(ayam eva)_, though exceedingly _(ati)_ diminished _(nikṛṣṭaḥ)_ <i>(and)</i> born _(jātaḥ)_ as the embodied _(ātmanā)_ Self of the elements _(bhūta)_ <i>(yet)</i> too _(api)_ <i>(remains)</i> Bhūteśa _(bhūteśaḥ)_, the Lord _(īśa)_ of the elements _(bhūta)_. Space _(gaganam)_, air _(anilaḥ)_ and _(ca)_ fire _(tejaḥ)_, water _(salilam)_ and _(ca)_ earth _(bhūmiḥ)_ <i>(are)</i> the five _(pañca)_ <i>(gross)</i> elements _(bhūtāni)_ ||18||

::: stanza 19

श्रोत्रादिकरणवेद्याः शब्दाद्यास्तानि वेदकान्येषाम्।     
वचनकरी वागासीत् पाणिः स्यात्करणभूत आदाने॥१९॥ {sans}

Śrotrādikaraṇavedyāḥ śabdādyāstāni vedakānyeṣām|     
Vacanakarī vāgāsīt pāṇiḥ syātkaraṇabhūta ādāne ||19||  {trans}

Sound _(śabda)_, etc. _(ādyāḥ)_ <i>(are)</i> knowable _(vedyāḥ)_ by the instruments _(karaṇa)_ <i>(beginning with)</i> the ear _(śrotra)_, etc. _(ādi)_. Those _(tāni)_ <i>(organs are)</i> the knowers _(vedakāni)_ of them _(eṣām)_. Speech _(vāk)_ is _(āsīt)_ the <i>(organ)</i> that produces _(karī)_ utterance _(vacana)_. The hand _(pāṇiḥ)_ is _(syāt)_ the instrument _(karaṇa)_ <i>(that has)</i> become _(bhūta)_ <i>(the organ)</i> for grasping _(ādāne)_ ||19||

::: stanza 20-21

गमनविसर्गानन्दत्रितये पादादिकं करणम्।     
गन्धवती भूमिः स्यादापः सांसिद्धिकद्रवास्तेजः॥२०॥ {sans}

उष्णस्पर्शमरूपस्पर्शो वायुरम्बरं सशब्दम्।     
षट्त्रिंशत्तत्त्वमयं वन्दे कौलं कुलातिगं शम्भुम्॥२१॥ {sans}

Gamanavisargānandatritaye pādādikaṁ karaṇam|     
Gandhavatī bhūmiḥ syādāpaḥ sāṁsiddhikadravāstejaḥ ||20||  {trans}

Uṣṇasparśamarūpasparśo vāyurambaraṁ saśabdam|     
Ṣaṭtriṁśattattvamayaṁ vande kaulaṁ kulātigaṁ śambhum ||21||  {trans}

<i>(For)</i> the triad _(tritaye)_ of locomotion _(gamana)_, excretion _(visarga)_ <i>(and)</i> enjoyment _(ānanda)_ <i>(there are)</i> the feet _(pāda)_, etc. _(ādikam)_ <i>(as their)</i> instruments _(karaṇam)_. Earth _(bhūmiḥ)_ is _(syāt)_ endowed _(vatī)_ with smell _(gandha)_. Water _(āpaḥ)_ <i>(is characterized by)</i> liquidity _(dravāḥ)_ <i>(which is its)</i> essential property _(sāṁsiddhika)_. Fire _(tejaḥ)_ ||20||

<i>(has)</i> heat _(uṣṇa)_ <i>(and)</i> touch _(sparśam)_. <i>Air</i> _(vāyuḥ)_ <i>(has)</i> formless _(arūpa)_ touch _(sparśaḥ)_. Space _(ambaram)_ <i>(is)</i> with sound _(sa-śabdam)_. I bow _(vande)_ before Śambhu _(śambhum)_, the Kaula _(kaulam)_ who transcends _(atiga)_ the Kula _(kula)_, <i>(who is)</i> composed _(mayam)_ of the thirty-six _(ṣaṭ-triṁśat)_ tattvas _(tattva)_ ||21||
